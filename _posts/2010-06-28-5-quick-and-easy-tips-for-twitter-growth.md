You want more followers on Twitter. You don’t know why you do, but you do.
Here are some rules to help you get started:

1) Add a profile picture and fill out your bio.

2) Share links. The vast majority of re-Tweets include links to something
else.

3) If you say “Please” and “Thank you” in your Tweets, you’re 3x more likely
to be re-Tweeted.

4) When you follow someone, there is a 30% chance they will follow you back.
Follow people.

5) Constantly invite people to follow you on Twitter (like I’m doing right
now… please [follow me](http://www.twitter.com/derekdac)!)
